A Little Q&A.

A few things about me, if you're curious.

I took the scenic route to UX.

I started in digital marketing, learning what catches people's attention. Then came product operations, where I became more curious about what happened after the click.

Somewhere along the way, I realized I was less interested in getting people to click a button, and more interested in why they needed to click it in the first place.

That's what led me to UX — and I haven't stopped asking questions since.

Good ideas get better together.

I'm a big believer in sharing ideas before they're fully polished. I like asking questions, sketching out possibilities, and figuring things out with others.

I bring my own point of view, but I'm not afraid to change my mind when someone brings a better one.

For me, good design is as much about the conversations along the way as the final outcome.

A little bit of everything.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably taking photos, listening to music on repeat, watching anime, making little things, or getting lost in fashion editorials.
